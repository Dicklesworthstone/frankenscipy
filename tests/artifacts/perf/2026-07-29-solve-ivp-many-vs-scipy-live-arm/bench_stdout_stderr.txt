Shape finding
-------------
The SciPy incumbent is flat at 0.853-0.908 seconds per batch because the
incumbent arm loops over trajectories serially. FrankenSciPy scales from
7.327 ms at one thread to a 1.822 ms minimum at 16 threads, then regresses to
6.317 ms at 128 threads. The competitive gap therefore widens through 16
threads and narrows after 16 because FrankenSciPy's per-invocation scoped-thread
coordination overtakes the small per-trajectory work. Retry a production
thread-cap lever only after adding a work-based cap that is independent of
machine thread count, then rerun this exact 1/2/4/8/16/32/64/128 sweep.
